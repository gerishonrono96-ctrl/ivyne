Dear Ivyne,

I don't know if you ever notice the little things about yourself that other people might notice.

The way a simple conversation can turn into a moment worth remembering. The way your presence can make an otherwise ordinary day feel a little less ordinary.

It's a funny thing, really. Sometimes you meet someone and, without any grand reason, you find yourself curious about them. You enjoy the little moments, look forward to the next conversation, and catch yourself smiling at something you remembered later.

Maybe it's nothing you can explain perfectly. Maybe not everything needs an immediate explanation.

I just thought some things deserved to be said in a way that doesn't interrupt the moment.

Just a thought, from someone who noticed. ♡
